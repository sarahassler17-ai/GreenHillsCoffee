Plantation Activities

Pruning
Removing unwanted branches to encourage healthy growth and improve coffee production.
Harvesting
Picking ripe coffee cherries from the coffee plants.
Drying
Reducing moisture in processed coffee beans to prepare them for storage and further processing.
